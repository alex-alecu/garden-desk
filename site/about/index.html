About Garden Desk

The long version.

What Garden Desk collects, how it runs, the model it ships with, and the hardware it needs. Everything the homepage leaves out.

Three promises, zero asterisks

We didn’t add a privacy setting.

We removed the thing it would protect you from. Nothing sent, nothing leaked. Nothing to configure, nothing to misconfigure.

01

Zero application telemetry

No analytics. No crash reports. No metrics. Not “off by default” — absent.

02

No cloud fallback

The model runs on your hardware. Hard task? It still runs on your hardware. There is no “then it uploads your file” step.

03

No runtime configuration

No providers, endpoints, model files, or sliders. It reads your hardware and decides. You point at a folder and ask.

Straight comparison

Private and finished.

Cloud is easy — you give up custody. Local keeps custody — you get a control panel. Read the third column.

Where work runs Common AI products Cloud or hybrid workflows often depend on remote services. Garden Desk Fully local and offline, with no silent cloud fallback.
Setup Common AI products Choose providers, model files, servers, endpoints, and memory settings. Garden Desk No runtime configuration. Hardware-aware defaults are automatic.
Who it is for Common AI products Interfaces are often organized around models and developer controls. Garden Desk An outcome-first workspace for ordinary, sensitive work.

Other local products also promise zero telemetry. What is different here is combining that with offline execution, bounded tools, and no model administration at all.

Privacy without an opt-out

We cannot track what we never collect.

Garden Desk tracks absolutely nothing. No pipeline to disable — none exists. The desktop works offline, and generated tools run in a sealed environment with no network interface.
