What makes Garden Desk different

  • One download

    The app, the model, and everything it runs on arrive together. Nothing to assemble, no keys, no service to sign up for.

  • Nothing to configure

    No model to choose, no settings to tune. Open it, point it at a folder, and ask for what you want.

  • Nothing tracked

    No analytics, no crash reports, not even a count of which features you use. There is nothing to opt out of.

  • No way online

    The agent works in a sealed workspace on your computer with no network connection at all. Your files stay put because there is no route out.
