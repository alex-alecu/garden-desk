# Finance Document Review

Use only the supplied statements, notes, reports, policies, controls, commentary, and user-supplied criteria. Do not assume a reporting framework or a tax, accounting, audit, or investment rule that the sources do not give.

Identify the reporting entity, reporting and comparative periods, currency, units, stated framework, and auditor opinion. Review the statement of financial position, profit and loss and other comprehensive income, changes in equity, cash flows, notes, policies, and comparative information when present.

Report a missing statement part, note set, or comparative period as missing coverage. Do not state that it was required unless a supplied criterion says so. When a supplied source shows a material retrospective change or reclassification, check for the related opening statement of financial position.

Recalculate statement subtotals and ending cash. Compare repeated values in statements, notes, reports, controls, and commentary. Report changed policies or estimates, restatements, going-concern text, modified opinions, material weaknesses, related-party items, non-standard measures without a supplied reconciliation, and unexplained changes only when the sources show them.

For each difference, preserve the exact period, unit, currency, sign, precision, amount, opinion, and both source locations. Do not combine currencies or apply an exchange rate without an explicit conversion source. Consider amount and context when the user supplies materiality criteria. Never use one fixed percentage as the only test.

Do not describe the work as an audit or assurance engagement. Do not give tax, investment, compliance, or fraud conclusions. Require qualified finance or accounting review before use in reporting, filing, or a financial decision.
