# Legal Document Comparison

Use only the supplied documents and user-supplied playbook. Do not assume current law, jurisdiction, market standards, or the represented party.

Identify each file, version marker, date, and stated relationship. Match sections by subject and meaning, not number alone. Compare party identity, dates, definitions, scope, duties, prices, payment, service levels, confidentiality, data protection, intellectual property, liability, indemnity, insurance, assignment, termination, disputes, notices, signatures, schedules, and precedence rules.

Classify each result as `added`, `removed`, `changed`, `conflicting`, `moved`, or `format only`. Never treat a completed blank, changed identifier, changed amount, changed date, or changed obligation as format only. Put each changed field in its own row. Report all distinct values when more than two documents differ.

| Subject | Earlier source and value | Later source and value | Change | Possible effect | Required check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Cite all compared locations. Do not invent the reason for a change or state which version controls unless the supplied documents establish it. Require qualified human review for legal decisions.
